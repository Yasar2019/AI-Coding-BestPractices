## Principles for roadmap decisions

A feature belongs here when it improves at least one of:

1. **learning** — people understand AI coding better;
2. **verification** — people can distinguish plausible output from trustworthy work;
3. **safety** — people can bound agent blast radius;
4. **evaluation** — people can measure their own workflows more rigorously;
5. **reproducibility** — claims and experiments can be checked by others.

The project will prefer small, verifiable increments over large features that cannot be maintained.
