# Case Studies: Evidence-Backed AI Outcomes

This document summarizes real-world and published findings on AI-assisted work. Each case links to the original source.

**Last updated:** 2025-12-19

---

## Case Study 1: Developer Productivity with GitHub Copilot

**Source:** GitHub Research Blog (2022)
- https://github.blog/2022-09-07-research-quantifying-the-productivity-gains-of-github-copilot/

**Summary:**
A controlled study compared developers using GitHub Copilot to a control group on programming tasks. The study reports **meaningful time savings**, with participants completing tasks substantially faster when Copilot was available.

**Key Insight:**
AI assistants can reduce time-to-completion for routine coding tasks, especially when the task is well-scoped.

## Case Study 2: Generative AI in Customer Support

**Source:** “Generative AI at Work” (NBER Working Paper)
- https://www.nber.org/papers/w31161

**Summary:**
A field experiment in a customer support environment found **productivity gains** and larger benefits for less-experienced workers when generative AI tools were used to assist with responses.

**Key Insight:**
Structured prompts and guardrails can improve quality and consistency in support workflows.

---

## Case Study 3: AI Assistance in Professional Writing Tasks

**Source:** “Experimental Evidence on the Productivity Effects of Generative AI” (Noy & Zhang, 2023)
- https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=4375283

**Summary:**
A randomized study on professional writing tasks reported **faster completion times** and **quality improvements** when participants used generative AI tools.

**Key Insight:**
AI can accelerate drafting and editing, but results improve most when users provide clear goals and constraints.

---

## Notes on Interpretation

- **Context matters.** Results vary by task complexity and user expertise.
- **Verification is essential.** Even strong results rely on human review and testing.
- **Check the full paper** for methodology, limitations, and detailed metrics.
